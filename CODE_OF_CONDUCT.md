# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the NomNom community respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and help contributors learn.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal insults
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing malicious code, credentials, or exploit details in public channels
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in an academic or professional environment

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, recurrence, and the safety of affected participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, project communication spaces, and when representing the NomNom project publicly.
